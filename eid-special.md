# Eid Special Recipe

## Ingredients
- 2 cups rice flour
- 1 cup date palm jaggery (gur)
- Grated coconut
- A pinch of salt

## Preparation
1. Prepare the rice flour mixture.
2. Add gur and grated coconut in layers.
3. Steam until cooked.
4. Serve during Eid.

## Serving Tips

- Serve the pitha warm.
- Garnish with extra grated coconut.
- Share with family during Eid.
